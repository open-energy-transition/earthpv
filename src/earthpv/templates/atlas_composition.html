Reading this breakdown

roofclf alone accounts for roughly four-fifths of the Best estimate — its own ≥ 400 m² replacement inside the density-calibrated domain and its sub-400 m² "central" estimate together dwarf every other source. Direct OSM mapping and TerraMind segmentation (ground-mount everywhere, plus rooftop outside the calibrated domain) split roughly the remaining fifth. SPPI's role in Best is deliberately small: it only appears in the out-of-domain extrapolation, a strict, explicitly-flagged judgement band with no calibration quadrat of its own — never blended into the in-domain roofclf estimate. SPPI's larger role is in Verified, the stricter floor, where roofclf-AND-SPPI agreement is almost as large as all of hand-mapped OSM combined.
