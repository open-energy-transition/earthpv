One instrument, two epochs

This page measures growth the way the evidence atlas measures capacity: the same segmentation checkpoint infers both a pre-boom (2021-10 to 2022-01) and a current dry-season composite, the same per-building classifier (roofclf) scores both epochs' reflectance, and both epochs run through the identical placement-split calibration. Each map is simply current minus pre-boom, per cell, component by component. The previous growth map diffed two different checkpoints and had no sub-400 m² instrument at all; it is superseded by this page.

Total growth

The atlas composition, differenced. Ground-mount (segmentation) plus ≥ 400 m² rooftop (roofclf where the density-calibrated domain covers the cell, segmentation elsewhere) plus sub-400 m² rooftop (roofclf), each estimated per epoch exactly as the evidence atlas estimates them, then differenced. Hand-mapped OSM capacity is excluded from both epochs: OSM features carry no install dates, so they cannot be assigned to an epoch, and both epochs' model components are de-duplicated against the same present-day OSM pull so that exclusion cancels in the diff.

Sub-400 m² (roofclf)

The half the old growth map could not see. roofclf scores every VIDA building's own reflectance in both epochs with one fitted model; flagged roofs convert to capacity through the same size- and density-stratified coverage-ratio and area-recall tables in both epochs. Those tables are measured on current-epoch mapping — applying them to 2021/22 assumes they transfer, which is untestable without pre-boom ground truth. That assumption's systematic part is shared by both epochs and cancels in the diff; it is why this page publishes differences of a fixed instrument, never a standalone historical level.

Rooftop ≥ 400 m²

roofclf inside the calibrated domain, segmentation outside it — the same split the evidence atlas uses, with the domain fixed to one shared national cell-density table so no cell switches instrument between epochs. Segmentation's rooftop estimate is recall- and precision-corrected per epoch with the identical calibration YAML.

Ground-mount (segmentation)

Segmentation is the only ground-mount instrument (roofclf has no footprint to classify there). Site area converts at the land constant with its own calibrated prior, per epoch, and the placement-split calibration prevents ground candidates borrowing rooftop precision. A plant that physically expanded since 2021/22 can be under-credited: OSM geometry replacement gives both epochs the same present-day footprint, biasing that plant's delta toward zero (conservative).

SPPI onset corroboration only

No model: a fixed five-band spectral formula on the same building's reflectance in both epochs. A building "onsets" when its SPPI crosses the has-PV threshold between the composites. Its MWp figure converts 100% of onset roof area with no precision weighting — an explicit outer ceiling from an independent mechanism, shown so the model-based maps can be checked against something that shares none of their machinery. Not part of the total.

Why both epochs use one checkpoint, and which one the first growth map's core flaw, and the deleted-checkpoint constraint+
A growth diff is only meaningful if the instrument is identical on both sides: any difference between two checkpoints' detection behaviour otherwise masquerades as growth. The first growth map (2026-08-06) violated this — its current epoch came from the production v3_combined_india checkpoint and its pre-boom epoch from an undocumented pk16085 variant. Both of those checkpoints have since been deleted from disk, so the only way to build a same-instrument pair today is the surviving v4_combined_all checkpoint (epoch=41), which scores both epochs here. Its current-epoch level differs slightly from the published evidence atlas's (which still rests on v3's outputs); the delta, not the level, is this page's product.
What the diff cannot see, and what it undercounts negative cells, undated OSM, footprint snapshots, radiometry+
Negative per-cell deltas are kept in the data (clamping would bias the national total up) but not colour-mapped; they are instrument noise from independently re-polygonizing two raster generations, and their total mass is reported in the footer as a scale for that noise. Hand-mapped OSM capacity is excluded from both epochs, so growth inside already-OSM-mapped installations is not measured here. VIDA building footprints are a present-day snapshot: a building constructed after 2021/22 exists in both epochs' building tables, with (correctly) no PV signal in the earlier one. Both epochs' composites predate the 2026-07-26 Collection-1 baseline-offset fix; per-cell fallback-scene usage differs between epochs, an unquantified radiometric confound shared with the published atlas's own composites. No composed credible interval yet: the calibration draws are shared between epochs and mostly cancel in the diff, but the residual is not priced — point deltas only.
