Hover any cell for its own numbers. For Low and Central, the colour and the totals shown are large rooftop PV plus small PV combined: small PV only inside the dashed outline, large PV everywhere. Outside the outline, small PV reads zero because there is no calibration evidence there yet, not because small solar is known to be absent, so those cells show large PV alone. For High, small PV is shown alone, not combined with large PV, since High is an explicit, uncalibrated ceiling and combining it with the project's main number would blur that distinction. For All-PV, the small-PV component is the same as Central's, but the large-PV component switches to every placement, ground-mount farms included, so the ring and the total both grow to match. This map's own 0.1° grid uses a slightly different origin than the survey that measured the checked area, so that area is drawn here as 0 cells rather than the 93 locations reported elsewhere; the totals are exact either way, since buildings were matched by location, not by cell name. Large PV is also drawn as a ring sized by capacity on every cell, regardless of the selected view.

What each view means

The satellite detector above only resolves installations of 400 m² or larger. Low and Central add large rooftop PV (the project's main, validated capacity number) to a small-PV estimate, so each is a genuine combined total. High is shown small PV only, on purpose, since it is an explicit, uncalibrated ceiling and combining it with the main number would blur that distinction. All-PV asks a wider question again: how much PV of any kind, roofs and ground-mount farms together. None of the small-PV components is a validated national total; read the scope note on each.

Low conservative

Large rooftop PV, plus roofclf AND SPPI agreeing on a building, restricted to the 93 cells whose building density matches the calibration quadrats. Requiring both detectors to agree raises measured precision (0.55 to 0.62 on held-out quadrats) at the cost of recall. The small-PV component is not a national figure on its own; it describes only the checked cells, while the large-PV component is national.

Central this project's own pick

Large rooftop PV, plus roofclf alone, same 93 checked cells. The small-PV instrument this project currently regards as its best domain-restricted estimate. Its own component is also not a national figure; the checked cells cover 19% of national buildings, concentrated in large cities.

High explicit ceiling, small PV only

Flat national precision (0.50) applied to every roofclf-flagged building countrywide, with no density restriction. This is a national number, but an explicitly uncalibrated one: the precision weight is known not to survive the shift from 9 urban and industrial calibration quadrats to 81.76 million mostly rural buildings. Treat it as an outer bound on plausibility, not an estimate; large rooftop PV is shown alongside for scale only, not added in.

All-PV wider question, ground-mount included

Central's small PV, plus large PV of every placement, ground-mount farms included, not just rooftops. Answers a different question from the other three: how much PV capacity exists at all, not how much of it sits on a roof. Ground-mount converts at a different constant (site area, not module area) and is the pipeline's most bug-prone component, the reason plausibility.py checks the ground-mount-to-rooftop ratio in the first place, so this view carries more composition risk than the other three, not less.

Large PV always shown

≥400 m² installations, detected by the segmentation model, recall-corrected. Rooftop-scope for Low, Central and High; every placement, ground-mount included, for All-PV, matching whichever total that view reports. This is the project's main, validated capacity number, added into the total on every combined view and shown for scale on High.
