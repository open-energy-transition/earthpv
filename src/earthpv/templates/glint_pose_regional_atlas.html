What this page is (and is not) a diagnostic re-cut, not a new estimate+

The underlying 2,000-target study was stratified by installation size, not region -- this is the first time it has been cut geographically. A targeted random top-up (401 more targets, drawn only from the provinces/latitude bands that were too thin to trust) was added afterward from the same source population (data/labels/pakistan_overpass_solar.parquet), same fetch mechanism, same date range, so the two pulls are directly poolable.

The top-up did not simply confirm the original small samples -- it's an honest re-measurement, and in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa and Balochistan the pooled rate came out lower than the original small sample suggested. That is exactly what a bigger, less noisy sample is supposed to do when the first draw was optimistic by chance; it is not a failure of the top-up.

Two territories remain data-limited by the underlying population, not by sampling effort. Azad Kashmir has 8 OSM-mapped solar installations in the entire country and Gilgit-Baltistan has 2 -- every one of them is already in this sample (a full census, not a sample), and no redraw changes that.

Two prior attempts at a regional density signal from glint both went negative. This is a different question -- not "does glint density track PV density regionally" but "does detection rate, validation rate, or fitted pose shift with latitude" -- so that negative history does not predict the answer here.
