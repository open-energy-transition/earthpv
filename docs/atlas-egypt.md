<div class="atlas-tier-alert atlas-tier-alert--bronze" role="note">
  <p><strong>Bronze atlas: smaller installations are missing.</strong> This map counts only installations mapped in OpenStreetMap and arrays the model can outline from space, roughly 400 m&sup2; (about 70 kWp) and larger. Household and small commercial rooftop systems, often most of a country's solar, are not included, because no area of Egypt has yet been mapped panel by panel to calibrate them. Read every figure here as a floor.</p>
