# Pakistan: national PV overview

Two independently-built views of Pakistan's solar footprint, in one page: the
sub-400 m<sup>2</sup> capacity bracket (large PV nationwide, plus four small-PV
estimates, switchable) and the glint-fitted panel-orientation survey. Switch tabs
inside the frame below.
