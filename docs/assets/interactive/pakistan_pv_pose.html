Why nothing measured crosses 180° -- and the mirrored half

Sentinel-2 crosses this latitude at a fixed ~10:30 local time, so across two full years of imagery the sun's azimuth at the moment of every overpass never once swings west of due south -- a panel facing southwest, west, or north cannot glint into this sensor no matter how long you wait. That's a sensor limit, not a property of Pakistani rooftops, so the dashed outline points beyond 180° are this same measured sample mirrored across the south axis. The residual shaded wedge (163° wide) is what stays unreachable even granting the mirror-symmetry assumption.

† A single spike can't be checked for self-consistency -- 25.1% of installations glint once (or on dates that disagree on a panel geometry) and so are correctly detected as "PV is probably here" but carry no orientation information.
