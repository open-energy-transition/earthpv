<div class="atlas-tier-alert atlas-tier-alert--silver" role="note">
  <p><strong>Silver atlas: one calibration area is mapped, not yet used.</strong> This map covers installations of roughly 400 m&sup2; (about 70 kWp) and larger, those mapped in OpenStreetMap and arrays the model can outline. Al Wehdah in Sana'a has been mapped panel by panel and declared complete, but no figure here is fitted to it yet, so there is still no estimate below that size, where household rooftop systems sit. Read every figure here as a floor.</p>
  <p><strong>You can help from your browser.</strong> The hollow diamonds on the map are candidate areas, among them 3 km farm areas for solar irrigation. Map every solar panel inside one in OpenStreetMap: a few more complete areas are what an estimate of Yemen's smaller installations would be fitted on.</p>
